# Security Policy

## Report a vulnerability

Do not disclose security vulnerabilities in public issues, pull requests, or
discussions.

Report potential vulnerabilities through the
[Upwork Bug Bounty Program](https://bugcrowd.com/upwork) or email
[security-reports@upwork.com](mailto:security-reports@upwork.com). Follow
Upwork's
[responsible disclosure guidance](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211067638-How-to-report-site-vulnerabilities)
and keep findings private until they are resolved.

Include clear reproduction steps, affected components, potential impact, and
any supporting logs or screenshots. Remove credentials, personal data, and
customer content from the report unless the secure reporting channel
specifically requests them.
